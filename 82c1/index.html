Introduction

In this post, I will discuss limits and continuity. There are 3 purposes for writing this post:

  1. To practise my academic writing.
  2. To write for fun.
  3. To expound some points that I was stuck on for a while at first when I was learning as an undergraduate in my first semester. It may help someone, or not, because the main purposes actually are 1&2.

I hope you can enjoy the read.

Limits of Sequences

First, I would like to note that all the discussions will be based on R\mathbb{R}R, and the discussions will not involve metric spaces. Before we continue, we need to state two axioms.

Axiom 1 (Archimedes’ axiom)

For every a,b>0a, b > 0a,b>0, there exists N∈NN \in \mathbb{N}N∈N, such that b<Nab < Nab<Na.

Axiom 2 (Cantor’s axiom)

an{a_n}an​ and bn{b_n}bn​ are two given sequences satisfying an<bn{a_n} < {b_n}an​<bn​ for every nnn, with associated intervals [an,bn][a_n,b_n][an​,bn​] nested and their lengths tending to zero. Then there exists a unique ccc such that ⋂n=1∞[an,bn]={c}\bigcap\limits_{n=1}^{\infty} [a_n, b_n]=\{c\}n=1⋂∞​[an​,bn​]={c}.

These axioms will be used later.

1.1Definition
For every ε>0\varepsilon > 0ε>0, there exists N∈NN \in \mathbb{N}N∈N such that for all n≥Nn \ge Nn≥N, ∣an−α∣<ε|a_n - \alpha| < \varepsilon∣an​−α∣<ε. We write an→αa_n \to \alphaan​→α as n→∞n \to \inftyn→∞, or

We call α\alphaα limit value and we say the limit of sequence {an}\{a_n\}{an​} is α\alphaα or {an}\{a_n\}{an​} converges to α\alphaα

Remark. If for every M>0M > 0M>0, there exists an N∈NN \in \mathbb{N}N∈N such that for all n≥N,an≥Mn \ge N, a_n \ge Mn≥N,an​≥M, then it means that the sequence {an}\{a_n\}{an​} does not converge(it diverges). 1.2 Theorem
If {an}\{a_n\}{an​} is convergent, the limit is unique.

Proof. Suppose that there are two different limit values, α\alphaα and β\betaβ. Since α≠β\alpha \ne \betaα=β, we let ε=∣α−β∣2\varepsilon=\frac{|\alpha - \beta|}{2}ε=2∣α−β∣​. Suppose that {an}\{a_n\}{an​} and {bn}\{b_n\}{bn​} converge to LLL, and also suppose that for every nnn, we have an<cn<bna_n < c_n < b_nan​<cn​<bn​.
